This Cookie Policy explains how Queen Reen uses cookies and similar technologies on queenreen.shop.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files stored on a device when a website is visited.

Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts, and device identifiers.

Categories of Technologies We May Use

Strictly Necessary

These technologies support website delivery, security and abuse prevention, load balancing, and network management. These may be used without optional consent where permitted by law.

Analytics

No analytics tool is currently installed on this site. This section will be updated with a named provider before one is added.

Functional

Functional technologies may remember preferences or support embedded features, such as the TikTok video player below.

Advertising and Affiliate Attribution

Affiliate networks, retailers, or advertising partners (currently TikTok Shop and LTK) may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute clicks or purchases when you follow a Shop link off this site. Queen Reen does not run cross-site advertising or behavioral tracking of its own.

Embedded Media

Embedded TikTok video (currently used on the Prequel Lip Visor review page, and on future review pages as they're built) may allow TikTok to collect information when the embed loads or is played.
