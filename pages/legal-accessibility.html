Accessibility Statement

Effective date: July 31, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 31, 2026

Queen Reen is committed to making queenreen.shop accessible to as many people as reasonably possible.

Our Approach

We aim to improve accessibility by using:

  • Semantic HTML
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation
  • Visible focus indicators
  • Meaningful headings
  • Descriptive link text
  • Alternative text for informative images
  • Sufficient color contrast
  • Captions or transcripts where reasonably available
  • Responsive layouts
  • Clear form labels and error messages

Our ongoing target is to align the website with generally recognized accessibility guidance, including relevant portions of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines.

This statement is not a claim that every page is currently fully conformant.

Third-Party Content

Some embedded videos, social-media content, affiliate tools, or third-party services may not be fully controlled by Queen Reen.

We encourage third-party providers to offer accessible experiences.

Feedback and Assistance

If you experience an accessibility barrier, email hello@queenreen.shop and include:

  • The page address
  • A description of the issue
  • The device and browser used, if known
  • The format or accommodation that would help

We will make reasonable efforts to respond and provide access or an alternative when feasible.

Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process.

We may review, test, and update the website as content and technology change.
