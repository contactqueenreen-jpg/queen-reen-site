Queen Reen respects intellectual-property rights and expects others to do the same.

Queen Reen Content

Unless otherwise stated, original content on queenreen.shop is owned by Fantasy Fruition, doing business as Queen Reen, or used under permission or license.

Protected material may include:

  • Photographs and portraits
  • Videos and audio
  • Voice recordings
  • Reviews, Diaries, guides, captions, and written copy
  • Logos and brand elements
  • Graphics and design assets
  • Original page layouts and compilations
  • Product curation and creative presentations
  • Social-media content
  • Downloadable resources

Copyright protection does not depend on whether a watermark is present.

No Permission by Default

Public availability does not place content in the public domain.

Unless a legal exception applies, you must receive written permission before:

  • Reposting full content
  • Reproducing photographs or video
  • Using content commercially
  • Editing or creating derivative works
  • Including content in advertisements, merchandise, or promotional material
  • Using content for artificial-intelligence training or synthetic media
  • Using Queen Reen's likeness, voice, or persona
  • Implying endorsement or affiliation

Credit, tagging, or linking back does not replace permission.

Permission Requests

Send permission requests to hello@queenreen.shop and include:

  • A link or clear description of the requested content
  • How it will be used
  • Where it will appear
  • Whether the use is commercial
  • Expected audience and distribution
  • Requested duration and territory
  • Proposed edits
  • Requested publication date

Silence is not permission.

Reporting Unauthorized Use of Queen Reen Content

Include:

  • The copied or misused content
  • The location of the unauthorized use
  • Screenshots and links
  • The date discovered
  • Your contact information
  • Any relevant account, host, marketplace, or platform details

Send reports to hello@queenreen.shop.

Copyright Complaints About Material on Queen Reen

If you believe material on the Queen Reen website infringes your copyright, send a written notice containing:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  3. Identification of the allegedly infringing material and enough information for us to locate it.
  4. Your address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the owner.

Send notices to: hello@queenreen.shop

Queen Reen has not registered a formal DMCA designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office. Until that changes, the email above is an informal contact point, not a registered safe-harbor agent — this notice should not describe it as one.

Counter-Notices

If material you submitted was removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe removal was mistaken, seek legal advice before submitting a counter-notice.

Any counter-notice must satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Repeat Infringement

Where applicable, Queen Reen may terminate or restrict access by repeat infringers.

Reservation of Rights

All rights not expressly granted are reserved.
